# Signal definitions

Use these to decide whether something counts.

## 1. New marketing leadership
**Counts:** an announced appointment of a CMO, marketing director, brand director or head of marketing, from the company, a trade title or a verified LinkedIn post.
**Doesn't count:** junior hires, interim cover under three months, or rumoured moves.

## 2. Rebrand or repositioning
**Counts:** a new logo, name, brand platform or strategy publicly announced or launched.
**Doesn't count:** a single seasonal campaign or a minor packaging refresh.

## 3. Agency review
**Counts:** a reported pitch, tender, review, roster change, or a confirmed split with an agency.
**Doesn't count:** speculation without a named source.

## 4. Funding or expansion
**Counts:** a confirmed funding round, acquisition, new market entry or major product launch.
**Doesn't count:** routine trading updates.

## 5. Campaign under pressure
**Counts:** a campaign withdrawn, an advertising regulator ruling, or sustained criticism in reputable outlets.
**Doesn't count:** a handful of negative social posts.
