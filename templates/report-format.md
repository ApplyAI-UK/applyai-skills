# Report format

Use this structure for every client, every run. Keep the fields in this order.

---

**[Client name] monitoring report · [period covered]**

**Headline**
One sentence on the most important development in this period.

**Key items** (most important first, maximum 8)

For each item:
- **Title** in plain English, with `Update` in front if it develops an item from the previous report
- **What happened** in one or two sentences
- **Why it matters** to this client in one sentence
- **Source** as a link to the original article, with `(headline only)` if you could not read the full piece
- **Type**: Client / Competitor / Topic
- **Channel**: Press / Social

**Worth watching**
Up to three items that are not urgent but may develop.

**Coverage snapshot**
- Items found: [number]
- Items kept after filtering: [number]
- Left out as repeats or unverified: [number]

**Notes**
Anything the team should know, such as sources that could not be opened, assumptions made on an unattended run, or a conflict with the client profile.

---

If nothing meets the bar, the whole report is:

> **[Client name] monitoring report · [period covered]**
> No developments met the threshold in this period.
